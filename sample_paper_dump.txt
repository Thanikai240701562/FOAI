[3] (Normal) [align: None]: Abstract: Traffic overload is a major problem, especially in urban regions. Changes in the weather and heavy traffic have a large impact on the flow of vehicles. This work analyzes traffic and weather data in different locations to study how weather and locality affect the flow of traffic. Data is collected and preprocessed, and weather-related features are combined with traffic datasets to identify meaningful patterns. By using machine learning algorithms such as Random Forest Regression, eXtreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) separately, this work predicts traffic conditions under different weather conditions and localities. Each model is compared based on its accuracy. The results show that the model becomes more accurate by adding locations and traffic data to the attributes. This provides better results. This study helps to improve solutions, which helps in suggesting routes based on traffic. The proposed model shows high performance. XGBoost obtained a Mean Squared Error (MSE) of about 73.34, with a testing Coefficient of Determination (R² score) of around 81.7% and a training R² of 93%, showing its performance under different weather conditions.
